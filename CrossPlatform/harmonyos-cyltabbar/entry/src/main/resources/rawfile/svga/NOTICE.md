The files are unchanged copies, renamed after the tab they show, used by the examples of CYLTabBarController
(docs/SVGA-Animation.md). They are 96 × 96 tab icons that play once (1 to 2 seconds) and keep their last frame.
The repository is published under Apache License 2.0; the icons look like they were taken from a shipping app, so
their original author is not named there. Replace them if their origin is ever disputed.
